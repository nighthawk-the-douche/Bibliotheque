# Optimized Cognitive Processing (OCP)

## <mark style="color:$danger;">DISCLAIMER</mark>

I am not a scientist, not a student of a leading university, not a neuroscientist, not a psychologist, not a philosopher. I'm just a guy with brains who wants to be able to use them better, for life and for work. Therefore, I do not know the topic completely deeply, I can make mistakes and omissions. If there are any comments, the Discord contact is on the introduction page of the site, I will be happy to discuss and discuss the omissions. But I think that since I am a simple person, it will be easier to understand what I mean, because I do not sell successful success, I do not tell how to live or a magical technique that will make you become the smartest person on earth. Any wisdom at first read seems elementary, although it takes years to truly understand it. Brains have a lot in common with muscles and physical exercises, so in theory it is like this: becoming physically strong is not difficult in theory, but difficult in practice. It's the same here.

Also, I understand that at first it may seem that 7 subsections are not enough for such a complex and extensive topic, but here I will say that it is better to be good and hone skills in 7 subtopics than to know a billion meaningless terms and techniques read from self-help bestsellers. Again, going back to the exercise analogy, seven exercises can get you a healthy strong body, and that's better than a million hyper-specific exercises.

## <mark style="color:$primary;">WHAT?</mark>

Learning is not just an abstract sponge for knowledge where we read something and immediately understood and remembered it. The whole process begins with various types of knowledge absorption, its structuring, processing, understanding and memorization. Each component has its own subcomponents or techniques that help in our goal. That's why I broke this idea into 7 components (and separately for the use of artificial intelligence for the purpose of learning, because here it can be easily used incorrectly).

| Topic                                               | Description                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <h3><strong>Metathinking</strong></h3>              | Ability to analyze and control your thoughts. Thinking about thinking.            |
| <h3><strong>Pattern Recognition</strong></h3>       | Recognizing similarities in any incoming data.                                    |
| <h3><strong>Selective Attention</strong></h3>       | Ability of our mind to focus on certain aspect faster and better than the others. |
| <h3><strong>Progressive Summarization</strong></h3> | Summarization of consumed materials, proper note making, Obsidian usage.          |
| <h3><strong>Timeboxing</strong></h3>                | Dividing learning time into Learn+Rest boxes. Pomodoro Technique.                 |
| <h3><strong>Feynman Technique</strong></h3>         | Techniques for understanding the topic more deeper, continuous self-checks.       |
| <h3><strong>Interval Learning</strong></h3>         | Repeating materials we've read for better remembering. Anki                       |
